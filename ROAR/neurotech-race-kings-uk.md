# The Neurotech Race: Is King’s getting inside the UK’s head?

**Published:** September 2026  
**Publication:** Roar News  
**Author:** Jorja Adamczyk

## Overview

A science and innovation article exploring King’s College London’s acquisition of the NeuroFUS 3D transcranial ultrasound system following £688,820 in BBSRC funding.

The article examines how Transcranial Ultrasound Stimulation (TUS) can be used to target deeper regions of the brain non-invasively, and how integrating NeuroFUS 3D with MRI could allow researchers to both stimulate and observe changes in brain activity.
