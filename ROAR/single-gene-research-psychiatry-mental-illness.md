# Single gene research forcing psychiatry to rethink mental illness

**ROAR Science Magazine | 21 February 2026**

## About this article

This article explores research linking the GRIN2A gene to psychiatric disorders and examines how the findings could challenge established ideas about the genetic basis of mental illness. It also considers the potential implications for precision psychiatry and future treatment approaches.
